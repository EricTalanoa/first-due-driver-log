## Promotional text (170 max)
Log your day in a few taps and keep a DOT-style duty chart ready for any inspection. Local days fill themselves in; LD days build step by step.

## Description
Driver Log is the duty log for First Due Movers drivers.

TODAY
• Add a local day in seconds: load and unload cities, clock times and lunch, and the DOT chart is filled in for you.
• See your day the way an officer does: off duty, sleeper, driving and on duty across 24 hours, with totals and the place of every change.

LONG DISTANCE
• Tap what you're doing as you go: Clock in, Start driving, Fuel stop, Lunch, Start unload, Switch drivers, Clock out.
• Each step asks for the time and place; miles are estimated from the places.
• Hours-of-service clocks show what's left of your 11 hours driving, 14-hour window, 30-minute break and 70-hour week.
• Pulled over? Inspection view shows today and the previous 7 days with your details.

HISTORY
• Every day by month, with LD trips outlined in red.

Your log is private to your account and also on the web. Export every entry as CSV, or delete your account and log at any time.

Driver Log is a personal logbook, not a certified electronic logging device (ELD).

## App Privacy (the "nutrition label")
Data used to track you: **No**. For each type below: *Linked to the user: Yes · Used for tracking: No · Purpose: App Functionality*.

| Data type | Why |
|---|---|
| Contact Info → Email Address | the account sign-in |
| Contact Info → Name | driver and co-driver names on the log |
| User Content → Other User Content | the log: times, places, miles, notes; truck and terminal details |
| Identifiers → User ID | the account's ID that keeps each driver's log separate |

Not collected: location, contacts, photos, browsing, purchases, diagnostics, usage data.
