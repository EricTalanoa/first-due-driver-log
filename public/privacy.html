FIRST DUE MOVERS · DRIVER LOG

Privacy policy

Effective November 27, 2026. Applies to the Driver Log iPhone app and website (first-due-driver-log-eric.netlify.app).

What Driver Log is

Driver Log is a duty and driving log for First Due Movers drivers. It keeps your hours, places and miles as a DOT-style record of duty status. It is a personal logbook, not a certified electronic logging device (ELD).

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, or the sign-in you choose (Apple or Google). With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email; we then only see Apple's relay address.
  • Details you enter: driver name, co-driver, carrier, truck and trailer numbers, home terminal and main office, and your home time zone.
  • Your log: the duty periods you record: times, duty status, the places you type or pick, miles and notes.
  • Tutorial: whether you have seen the in-app tutorial.

Driver Log does not collect your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers, has no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking.

How it is used

Only to run the app: to save your log, show it on your phone and the website, and produce the chart, totals and inspection view. We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your account and log are stored with Supabase (our database provider) in the United States. Each driver can read only their own log; this is enforced by the database, not just the app. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS).

Your choices

  • Export: download every entry as a CSV file from Settings (iPhone) or the website.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account and every log entry, on the web and in the app.
  • Questions or requests: email erictalanoa@gmail.com.

Children

Driver Log is for working drivers and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
