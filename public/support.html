Common questions

I forgot to tap a step. On the LD tab, enter it now and set the time to when it happened. To fix a finished day, use Fill in or fix a day, or tap the line on the chart.

The miles look off. Miles are estimated from the places on each side of a drive. Type the real miles when you save the stop, or edit the drive afterwards.

I started over by mistake. Clear day can't be undone. Re-enter the day with Fill in or fix a day.

What do I show at an inspection? On the LD tab, open Inspection view: today and the previous 7 days with your details.

Can I see the tutorial again? iPhone: Settings → Show tutorial again, or the ? on the LD tab.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. Everything is deleted permanently. See the privacy policy.

Driver Log is a personal logbook, not a certified ELD. Drivers who keep logs more than 8 days in any 30-day period generally need an ELD.
